# Information about my favorite movie - Dune: Part 2

**House Atreides is in complete ruins from the surprise attack of the combined Harkonnen and Sardaukar force. And now Paul Atreides, his mother, and the missing Gurney Halleck are the only surviving elements left of the once mighty house. The Harkonnens quickly rush to regain control of the city and restore it's spice production to regain losses from the cost of assembling the massive operation that took down House Atreides, all the while Paul and his mother escaped the carnage and managed to take refuge with the Fremen as they lick their wounds.**
